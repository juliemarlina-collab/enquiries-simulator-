Unit 2: Enquiries in Daily Life

Interactive Set Induction Phase (15-Minute Class Hook)

Why does polite enquiry structure matter?

Every day, you ask for information or make requests in English. In professional and daily situations, how you structure your request changes how people respond to you!

Today we will explore how to make Courteous, Clear, and Concise inquiries in daily life. For example:

Direct/Messy Request (Could be impolite) Polite/Structured Inquiry (Very Clear!)
"Tell me where the library is." "Excuse me, could you tell me where the library is?"
"I don't understand. Explain it again." "Could you please clarify this step for me?"
"I want a badminton court on Friday." "I would like to inquire about booking a badminton court."
